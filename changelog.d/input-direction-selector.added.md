**The game has an input library, starting with keys, gamepads and a shared steering rule.** A new portable library, `libs/input`, maps the arrow keys, W A S D and the gamepad d-pad to directions, and Space or Start, Esc or Select or B, and Enter or A to pause, menu and confirm. A direction selector turns two axis values into at most one direction, with a dead band and a hold against flicker. The left stick uses it, and tilt will use it next.
