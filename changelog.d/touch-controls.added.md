**On a phone you steer by tilting it, by swiping, or with an on-screen d-pad.** Choose which on the pause dialog (tilt is the first choice, and a phone with no way to feel its tilt is steered by swiping). With tilt, the way you hold the phone when a game begins is neutral, Recalibrate tilt on the pause dialog takes it again, and a small indicator under the board shows the tilt, the dead zone and the angle that turns the snake. Inverted axes in the settings turn it the other way. A pause button sits at the top of the game, since a phone has no Escape key. Translated into the seven languages (machine translations, not read by a speaker). Not yet tried on a real phone.
