**On a phone or a tablet.** Steer by tilting it (with a short calibration, and a choice of how sensitive), by swiping, with an on-screen d-pad or with an on-screen joystick that reads eight ways. The phone taps when the snake eats and knocks when it crashes, and the game keeps inside the camera cut-out and the home bar. An iPhone and iPad version goes out through TestFlight; the Android package builds but has not been tried on a device.
