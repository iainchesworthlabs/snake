**The game speaks French, Spanish and German.** Every screen is translated, and the game shows the language of the computer or phone, or the one named with `--language` (for example `snake --language de`). The translations are made by machine and have not been read by someone who speaks the language. Long words no longer run out of buttons and cards: labels and headings are set smaller to fit, and the detail under a mode wraps to two lines.
