**The game takes the first language you prefer that it has, and any English is English.** A phone that lists English (UK), German and French showed German. The game now looks down your list of preferred languages, in order, matching on the language alone: English (UK), English (Australia) and every other English are English, and so on for the other languages, with Simplified Chinese for Chinese unless the tag says Traditional. About shows what it chose, and from which preference.
