**Tilt can be made quicker or gentler.** The pause dialog has a Tilt sensitivity row: Gentle turns the snake when the phone is tipped 24 degrees, Normal at 18 and Quick at 12. It is kept with the other settings. Tapping the tilt indicator three times shows what the phone's sensor says, for a bug report.
