**The game speaks Hebrew and Yiddish, and reads from right to left.** In these languages the screens are mirrored: the back button, the title's buttons, the switches, the score and the best, and the leaderboard lines swap sides, and text starts on the right. The board, the arrow keys and the letters of the name stay as they are. The translations are made by machine and have not been read by someone who speaks the language; Yiddish especially needs a reader.
