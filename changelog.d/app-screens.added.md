**Title, pause and game over screens.** The game now opens on a title screen where you choose Easy or Classic and press Play. Portals and Hot potato are listed with the score that unlocks them in Classic (2,000 and 3,000) and cannot be started yet. Pausing shows a dialog with Resume, Restart and Quit to menu. When a game ends, a screen shows the final board, your score against your best for that mode and how many points short you were, and offers Play again or Menu. The arrow keys move between the buttons and Enter presses them. The best scores last until the game is closed; saving them comes with the leaderboard.
