**Settings.** The title has a Settings button with switches for sound, music and reduced motion (and inverted axes on a touch screen), and a reset for the Easy and Classic leaderboards that asks before it deletes anything. The pause dialog has the sound and music switches too and, on a touch screen, a choice of tilt, swipe or d-pad and a Recalibrate tilt button. With reduced motion on, the food stops bobbing. Sound and music do nothing yet: the audio comes later.
