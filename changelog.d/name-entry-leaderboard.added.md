**Name entry and the leaderboard.** A game that makes the top 10 now offers Save score on the game over screen. Name entry shows the place the score will take, twelve name slots, your last three names as one-tap shortcuts and a letter grid; the keyboard types the name directly (Backspace takes a letter off, Enter saves). Saving opens the leaderboard on that mode with your line in coral. The title has a Leaderboard button, and the leaderboard has a tab for Easy and Classic, medal tiles for the first three places and a Play button. Menu on the game over screen lets an unsaved score go.
