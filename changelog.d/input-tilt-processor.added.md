**Tilting the device can steer, in the input library.** The tilt processor smooths accelerometer readings, treats the pose the player holds as neutral, and sets a direction when the device is tipped 18 degrees (it lets go below 12, and on a diagonal the axis in control keeps priority until the other is 5 degrees larger). Sensor readings come through a small interface, and a fake source plays back recordings, so tilt is tested without a device.
