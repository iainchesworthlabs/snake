<h1>Accessibility</h1>
<p class="icl-lead">This is what is in place. It lists only what has been checked in the game's code.</p>
</div></section>

<section class="sk-text">
<h2>The game</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ways to steer.</strong> On a phone or tablet: tilt, swipe, an on-screen d-pad or an on-screen joystick. On a computer: the keyboard or a gamepad. You pick the one that suits you.</li>
<li><strong>Speed.</strong> Easy is the slowest mode, and the snake only speeds up as it eats.</li>
<li><strong>Reduced motion.</strong> A switch in Settings turns off the shake and the bobbing food. On a first run it follows your device's setting.</li>
<li><strong>Sound.</strong> Switches for sound and music, and a volume.</li>
<li><strong>Right to left.</strong> Hebrew and Yiddish are laid out right to left.</li>
<li><strong>Names for screen readers.</strong> The buttons, switches and mode cards carry names and roles for assistive technology in the game's code.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Not tested yet</h2>
<ul>
<li>The game has not been tried with VoiceOver, TalkBack or another screen reader, so we do not say it works with one.</li>
<li>The game draws its own text. We have not checked whether it follows your device's text size.</li>
<li>The colours of the game have not been measured against a contrast standard.</li>
</ul>
<h2>This site</h2>
<ul>
<li>These pages work without JavaScript.</li>
<li>They follow your device's light or dark setting.</li>
<li>The site's colours are checked against the WCAG 2.2 AA contrast levels when the site is built.</li>
</ul>
<p>If something does not work for you, tell us on the <a href="../support/">Support</a> page.</p>
</section>
