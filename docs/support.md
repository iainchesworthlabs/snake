# Support

Snake is a Snake game with four modes. This page says where to get help.

## Before you ask

- [How to play](how-to-play.md) covers the modes, scoring and the controls.
- The [FAQ](faq.md) has short answers.

## How to get in touch

Open an issue on the project's page:
[github.com/iainchesworthlabs/snake/issues](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/snake/issues).
You need a free GitHub account to do that.

Support is through GitHub issues only. There is no email address.

## What to tell us

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- The version of the game. Open **Settings**, then **About**. Tap the version five times to show the details for a bug report.
- The kind of device and its system version, and how you were steering (tilt, swipe, d-pad, joystick, keys or a gamepad).
- A screenshot, if it helps.

## What the game does not do

The game has no accounts and sends nothing to the project (on an iPhone or iPad it can send your scores to Game Center, which is Apple's, if you are signed in), so nobody on the project can see your scores or look up your settings. See [Privacy](privacy.md).
