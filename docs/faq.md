# FAQ

Short answers.

## Getting it

**Where can I get Snake?**
: For Linux, macOS and Windows, from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/snake/releases). An iPhone and iPad version is on its way to the App Store. See [the home page](index.md#download).

**Is there an Android version?**
: The package builds, but it has not been tried on a device, so there is no download.

**Does it cost anything?**
: The downloads on GitHub are free. The App Store price is not set yet.

**Does it have ads or in-app purchases?**
: No. The game has no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

## Playing

**How do I open Portals and Hot potato?**
: Score 2,000 in Classic for Portals and 3,000 for Hot potato. They stay open once you have them.

**How do I shed the bomb in Hot potato?**
: Press the Shed button, Enter on a keyboard, or A on a gamepad. See [How to play](how-to-play.md).

**Why did the game pause?**
: A game pauses by itself when its window loses the focus, and on a phone when the screen turns. Resume it from the pause screen.

**The snake did not turn when I pressed a key.**
: A turn straight back on yourself is ignored. Two quick turns in a row are both kept, up to two.

**Can I play with a gamepad?**
: Yes, on a computer. Phones and tablets do not read gamepads yet.

## Your data

**Where are my scores?**
: In a file in the game's data folder on your device. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

**Does the game send anything anywhere?**
: No. It does not use the network.

**How do I clear a leaderboard?**
: Settings, Reset a leaderboard. It asks you to confirm.

## Languages

**Is it in my language?**
: English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Hebrew and Yiddish. The game picks from your device's languages. The translations are made by machine and have not been read by someone who speaks each language. If one is wrong, tell us on [Support](support.md).
