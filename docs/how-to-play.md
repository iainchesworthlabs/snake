<h1>How to play</h1>
<p class="icl-lead">Eat the food, grow, and do not run into yourself.</p>
</div></section>

<section class="sk-text">
<h2>The aim</h2>
<p>The snake moves all the time. You choose which way it turns. Each blueberry it eats makes it longer and scores points. The game ends when its head runs into its own body, or into a wall (in every mode but Easy). The snake starts short, in the middle, heading right. It gets faster as it eats.</p>
<p>You cannot turn straight back on yourself. The game checks each turn against the way the snake will be heading by then, so a quick up-then-left while heading right does not fold it back.</p>

<h2>The modes</h2>
<dl class="sk-qa">
<dt>Easy</dt>
<dd>The slowest. The walls wrap around: leave on one side and you arrive on the other.</dd>
<dt>Classic</dt>
<dd>Faster. A wall ends the game. So it does in Portals and Hot potato.</dd>
<dt>Portals</dt>
<dd>Two pairs of portals sit on the board. Step into one and you come out of its twin, going the same way. It opens when you score 2,000 in Classic.</dd>
<dt>Hot potato</dt>
<dd>A bomb food now and then. Eat it for 100 points and the bomb rolls down your body towards your tail. Press Shed to drop it, and the part of your tail behind it, before it goes off. It opens when you score 3,000 in Classic.</dd>
</dl>
<p>Once a mode is open, it stays open.</p>

<h2>Scoring</h2>
<ul>
<li>Each food is worth 10 points times your combo.</li>
<li>Eat again within 20 steps of the last food and the combo goes up by one, up to five. Wait longer and it goes back to one.</li>
<li>Every fifth food is followed by a bonus item worth 50 points. It does not stay long. It does not raise the combo, but it keeps the combo going.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Controls</h2>
<h3>On a computer</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Steer:</strong> the arrow keys, or W, A, S and D.</li>
<li><strong>Pause and resume:</strong> Space.</li>
<li><strong>Menu:</strong> Esc.</li>
<li><strong>Shed, in Hot potato:</strong> Enter.</li>
</ul>
<p>With a gamepad plugged in or paired, the d-pad or the left stick steers, Start pauses, Select or B opens the menu, and A presses the button that has the focus (and sheds). The game pauses by itself when its window loses the focus.</p>
<h3>On a phone or tablet</h3>
<p>Choose one of four ways to steer: tilt the device, swipe, an on-screen d-pad, or an on-screen joystick that reads eight directions. Tilt starts with a short calibration and has three sensitivities. The device taps when the snake eats and knocks when it crashes. Hold the device upright or on its side. The screen is held still during a game, so tilting never turns it.</p>

<h2>The leaderboard</h2>
<p>Each mode keeps its own top 10 on your device. If your score is on it, the game asks for a name of up to 12 letters (A to Z and the space). The three names you used most recently are there as one-tap shortcuts. In Settings you can reset the leaderboard of one mode, after a confirmation.</p>
<h2>Achievements</h2>
<p>There are twelve achievements, such as eating a bonus item, going through a portal or opening a mode. A game that you finish can earn them, and the game over screen names any that it did. A game that you leave from the pause menu earns nothing. They are kept on your device. The Achievements button on the title screen opens the list: a tick marks the ones you have earned and a padlock the ones you have not, and a tap or the arrow keys show what each one takes. On an iPhone or iPad, a player who is signed in to Game Center also has a Game Center button there that opens Game Center's list.</p>
